Exhibition Program

The 1st International Conference and Exhibition on Green Jobs for a Net Zero Future

October 26-28, 2026 | HUST, Hanoi, Vietnam

Exhibition Features

The exhibition is organized into four functional zones, bringing together academia, industry leaders, and innovators to facilitate knowledge exchange and promote sustainability pathways.

Zone 01

Education Hub

  • Micro-credentials and modular learning
  • Train-the-Trainer (ToT) and replication models
  • ASEAN TVET-HE alignment initiatives
Zone 02

Industry Showcase

  • Green industry tools and platforms
  • Green technologies and ESG solutions
  • Internship and employment pathways
Zone 03

Youth Talent Arena

  • Student projects and demonstrations
  • Hackathons, innovation challenges
  • Social innovation and startup pitches
Zone 04

Institutional Solutions Zone

  • Tools for curriculum integration
  • Institutional reform and scaling models

Exhibition Highlights

10+ Exhibitors From ASEAN and EU regions, spanning across 7 core sectors.

These clinics are scheduled alongside conference sessions and embedded within the exhibition space to maximize participation.
